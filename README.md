# My QuizDock quizzes

Quizzes made with [QuizDock](https://github.com/quizdock/quiz-dock), shared through the QuizDock community store.

## Publish a quiz

1. In QuizDock, open the quiz and choose **Export for publication**. You get a `.quizdock.zip` file.
2. Unzip it on your computer: you get a folder.
3. Here, open the **quizzes** folder, click **Add file → Upload files**, drag the whole folder onto the page, then **Commit changes**.

To update a quiz, upload its new folder over the old one (delete the old folder if its name differs). To remove it, delete its folder. The store builds the zips itself: never upload a zip.

The full guide: [publish your quizzes](https://github.com/quizdock/quiz-store/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
